Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Discnct is built to run entirely on your phone. This page explains what the app can access, what it does with it, and what — if anything — ever leaves your device.

What Discnct accesses, and why

  • Accessibility Service — used only to detect when a reel/shorts-style surface (e.g. Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts) is on screen, and to detect when a blocked app is in the foreground. Discnct does not read, log, or transmit the content of your screen, your typing, or any other app's data. This runs entirely on-device.
  • Usage Access — used to show you your own in-app usage stats (time spent, games played) inside Discnct's Stats tab. This data never leaves your device.
  • Display over other apps — used to show the block screen (the hold-to-unlock / play-a-game screen) on top of a blocked app or reel surface.
  • Query all installed apps — used to list your installed apps so you can choose which ones to add to your block list, and (in Total Disconnect / Level 3) which ones stay reachable from Discnct's restricted home screen.

What we collect

Nothing. Discnct has no account, no login, and no analytics or tracking SDK. Your block list, settings, and usage stats are stored locally on your device (via Android's DataStore) and are deleted when you uninstall the app. We never see them.

Payments

Discnct offers two separate, optional payments, both handled entirely by third parties — Discnct's own servers never see or store your payment details:

  • "Buy us a Coffee" — a voluntary tip that unlocks nothing. Paid via UPI directly through your UPI app, or by card via Razorpay Checkout in your browser.
  • Level 2+ unlock — a one-time purchase to keep App Blocker + Games and Total Disconnect after their 12-day free trial. Processed entirely through Google Play Billing, per Play Store policy.

Third parties

Razorpay (payment processing for the optional coffee tip) and Google Play Billing (the Level 2+ purchase) are the only third parties involved, and only when you actively choose to pay. Neither receives any data from Discnct beyond what's needed to process that one transaction.

Children

Discnct is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone, child or adult — see "What we collect" above.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
