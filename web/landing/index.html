Philosophy

Friction, not force.

Most blockers are a locked door: on or off, all or nothing, one password away from defeat. Discnct is built on a different bet — that the right amount of resistance, matched to how bad the habit is, works better than a wall you'll just tear down.

01

You choose the level

Start surgical. Escalate only when a lighter touch stops being enough — never further than you need.

02

No dark patterns

No streaks to protect, no guilt copy, no ads. It either helps you scroll less or it doesn't — nothing else it's optimizing for.

03

Reversible, on purpose

Every level can be turned back off. The goal is a habit you don't need the app for anymore, not a cage.

How it works

Three levels, escalating on purpose.

Each level is a complete, independent tool — turn on just the one that matches your problem, or stack all three.

LEVEL 01

Reel Blocker

The moment a Reels or Shorts feed opens inside an app you've guarded, Discnct bounces you straight back out — no block screen, nothing to dismiss. The rest of that app, DMs, search, your feed, stays completely usable. Only the endless vertical scroll disappears.

LEVEL 02

App Blocker + Games

Whole apps get shut behind a block screen. Hold a button for 30 seconds to earn 5 minutes in, or beat a quick puzzle — Wordle, 2048, Minesweeper, a chess mate-in-one — to earn more. Enough friction to make you ask "do I actually want this," not enough to be un-openable in a real emergency.

LEVEL 03

Total Disconnect

Discnct replaces your home screen entirely, showing only a minimal grid of the apps you've explicitly allowed through — essentials like phone, messages and camera by default. Everything else still routes through the Level 2 block screen. The nuclear option, for when the first two aren't enough.
